# REQ-0015: Adaptive draft length from measured acceptance and confidence

- Status: experimental

## Requirement

Speculative decoding must not require a hand-set draft length per card.
For each (target, drafter) pair, the draft length is chosen from the
decayed measured acceptance of that drafter and, when the drafter reports
its top-1 confidence, the current step's confidence, by the expected
tokens per unit of verify-pass time. When drafting is expected to cost
more than it earns, the policy proposes 0 (no draft) for that step.

## Acceptance

- `kindi/draft_policy.py` proposes k in 0..kmax (at most 7): high measured
  acceptance (above 0.9) picks the longest draft, low acceptance (below
  0.15) picks none, and a low step confidence never proposes a longer
  draft than a high confidence on the same history (pinned by
  `tests/test_draft_policy.py`).
- The policy is pure host arithmetic: it never changes a token, only how
  many the drafter proposes.
- Adoption in `kindi/spec.py` (driving the T>1 verify passes) passes its
  gate: 4/4 greedy exact against the fixed-k rows, and no tok/s
  regression on the 2B and 27B rows in `docs/MTP.md` at the same context.
  The 2B row passed on the RTX 4060 Ti (2026-11-15, `bench-logs/req0015-gate/`);
  the 27B row remains pending (no 27B pack and no second card on this host).
- A generalized plug-in harness (an arbitrary draft GGUF paired to any
  target) is a separate requirement: it needs a second draft engine and
  the same gate, and must not be assumed from this one.

## Evidence

- `kindi/draft_policy.py`: `DraftPolicy` (decayed acceptance, confidence
  scaling, cost-table pick). Pure-python, tested.
- `tests/test_draft_policy.py`: pick-no-draft pick-longest, kmax bound,
  confidence ordering, stats, unknown-cost fallback, `limit_k` clamp rules.
- `kindi/spec.py`: `KINDI_DRAFT_POLICY=1` attaches a `DraftPolicy` to the target
  engine; it only shortens the engine's base k (never lengthens it) and records
  every verify as `observe(kept, drafted)`. Tokens are decided by verify()
  alone, so exactness cannot change.
- **GPU adoption gate (2B, `KINDI_SPEC=draft2b`, `KINDI_TC=1`, `KINDI_TP=1`, ctx 1024,
  RTX 4060 Ti):** fixed-k: 4/4 PASS, 318.2 tokens/s. Policy on: 4/4 PASS,
  368.4 tokens/s (+15.8%, no regression). Evidence: `bench-logs/req0015-gate/`.
  The fixed-k rows in `docs/MTP.md` remain the baseline; the 27B row is pending.
